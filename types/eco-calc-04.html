Calculating price elasticity of supply (PES)

Calculation

Price elasticity of supply (PES) shows how much quantity supplied responds to a price change. Divide the % change in quantity supplied by the % change in price.

1

Find PES, or the change

Decide first, then tap the ? to check.

3 steps to find

Price rose, so the farm supplies more. Find PES.

  1. Top: quantity +6%
  2. Bottom: price +8%
  3. PES = 0.75

Top: quantity +6%

Why
Quantity supplied always goes on top.

Bottom: price +8%

Why
Price change goes underneath.

PES = 0.75

Working
6 ÷ 8 = 0.75
Meaning
Below 1, so supply is price inelastic.

Exam tipNo % sign on PES.

0 of 3 found

3 steps to find

PES is given. Find the % change in quantity supplied.

  1. PES: 1.5
  2. Price falls 4%
  3. Change = −6%

PES: 1.5

Why
Above 1: supply is price elastic.

Price falls 4%

Why
Keep the minus sign.

Change = −6%

Working
1.5 × −4% = −6%
Meaning
Quantity supplied falls by 6%.
0 of 3 found
2

The calculation

PES = % change in quantity supplied ÷ % change in price

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find %ΔQs:%ΔQs = PES × %ΔP

To find %ΔP:%ΔP = %ΔQs ÷ PES

  • PES has no % sign.
  • PES is positive: a fall ÷ a fall is positive.

Try itPrice rises 12% and quantity supplied rises 15%. Find PES.

  1. 15 ÷ 12 = 1.25
  2. PES = 1.25: above 1, so elastic.

Exam question

The price of olive oil rose by 6.5%. Quantity supplied rose by 3.9%. Calculate the PES to two decimal places. (2 marks)

5

The method

  1. Write both changes

    Note the % changes, with their signs.

  2. Supply on top

    Quantity supplied ÷ price.

  3. Divide or multiply

    If PES is given, PES × price change.

  4. Round and judge

    Round as asked. Above 1 elastic, below 1 inelastic.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Upside down

    Price on top gives the wrong value.

  • ✗
    A % sign

    PES is a number with no unit.

  • ✗
    A minus PES

    Two falls give a positive answer.

Worked explanation

  1. Price rose by 5.4% and quantity supplied rose by 4.7%.
  2. PES = 4.7 / 5.4 = 0.8703...
  3. Round to two decimal places: 0.87.
  4. It is below 1, so Greg's supply is price inelastic.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

PES = % change in quantity supplied / % change in price = 4.7% / 5.4% = 0.8703 = 0.87 (to 2 d.p.).

How the marks are earned

Two marks. The first comes from writing 4.7 divided by 5.4. The second comes from the answer 0.87 to two decimal places. The right answer by itself also gets both marks.
