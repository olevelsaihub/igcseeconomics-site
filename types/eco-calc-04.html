Knowledge card

Calculating price elasticity of supply (PES)

Calculation
  • Price elasticity of supply (PES) measures how much quantity supplied responds to a change in price.
  • Formula: PES = % change in quantity supplied / % change in price.
  • PES is positive, because price and quantity supplied move in the same direction (a fall divided by a fall is positive).
  • Above 1 is price elastic supply; below 1 is price inelastic supply.
  • Rearranged: % change in quantity supplied = PES x % change in price.

Before you begin

  • Meaning of supply
  • Dividing decimals
  • Rounding to two decimal places
1

The method

  1. Write down the % change in quantity supplied and the % change in price, with signs.

    Why: Both falls or both rises give a positive PES.

  2. Put quantity supplied on top and price on the bottom.

    Why: The formula only works this way round.

  3. Divide; if PES is given instead, multiply PES by the price change.

    Why: Covers both the 'find PES' and 'find the change' versions.

  4. Round to the decimal places asked; PES has no % sign, but a change in quantity does.

    Why: The answer mark needs the correct value and form.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Putting the price change on top of the fraction.
  • ✗
    Giving PES a % sign, which can cost the answer mark.
  • ✗
    Giving a negative PES when both changes are falls.
  • ✗
    Not rounding to two decimal places when asked.

Common mistakes: Inferred, not measured. Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

Worked explanation

  1. Price rose by 5.4% and quantity supplied rose by 4.7%.
  2. PES = 4.7 / 5.4 = 0.8703...
  3. Round to two decimal places: 0.87.
  4. It is below 1, so Greg's supply is price inelastic.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

PES = % change in quantity supplied / % change in price = 4.7% / 5.4% = 0.8703 = 0.87 (to 2 d.p.).

How the marks are earned

Two marks. The first comes from writing 4.7 divided by 5.4. The second comes from the answer 0.87 to two decimal places. The right answer by itself also gets both marks.
