The WTO promotes freer trade: it hosts negotiations to cut barriers, sets and monitors trade rules, and settles disputes. It does not lend money.

The method

  1. Name a role

    Negotiations, rules, settling disputes, or cutting protectionism.

  2. Say how

    Brings members together, monitors, rules on complaints.

  3. Show the result

    Barriers fall, so more trade.

  4. Apply

    Use the countries or dispute in the case.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Lending

    The WTO does not lend money.

  • ✗
    Raising barriers

    It aims to lower them.

  • ✗
    Bare role

    Link it to more trade.

Worked explanation

  1. Data: in 2017 the US government wanted a 300% tariff on aircraft imported from Bombardier, a Canadian firm.
  2. Role: the WTO sets trade rules and settles disputes.
  3. How: Canada can complain to the WTO, which judges whether the tariff breaks its rules.
  4. Effect: the tariff is removed or not imposed, so trade continues freely.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

One way is through the WTO's rules and its role in settling trade disputes. If the US places a 300% tariff on Bombardier aircraft from Canada, Canada can take a complaint to the WTO, which acts as a referee and decides whether the tariff breaks the trade rules both countries have agreed to. If it does, the US can be required to remove or reduce the tariff, so Canadian aircraft can continue to be sold in the US and trade between the two countries stays free.

How the marks are earned

1 mark: a WTO role (setting trade rules and settling disputes). 1 mark: development (the WTO judges the complaint and can require the tariff to be removed). 1 mark: context (the 300% tariff on Canadian Bombardier aircraft).
