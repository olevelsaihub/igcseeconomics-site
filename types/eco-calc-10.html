Knowledge card

Profit or loss from revenue and costs

Calculation
  • Profit = total revenue - total cost.
  • If the answer is negative, the firm makes a loss.
  • Per unit: profit per unit = selling price - cost per unit (add all the unit costs first).
  • Total revenue = price x quantity sold.

Before you begin

  • Total revenue
  • Total cost
  • Negative numbers
1

The method

  1. Find revenue: price x quantity, or just the price if the question is per unit.

    Why: Profit starts from what the firm earns.

  2. Find the matching cost: total cost, or the sum of all costs per unit.

    Why: Revenue and cost must be on the same basis.

  3. Subtract cost from revenue and write the working.

    Why: Earns the working mark.

  4. Give the amount with currency and say profit if positive, loss if negative.

    Why: The label shows you read the sign correctly.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Forgetting to subtract costs and giving revenue as profit.
  • ✗
    Subtracting only one of several unit costs.
  • ✗
    Calling a positive answer a loss (or the reverse).
  • ✗
    Multiplying costs by price, as in some wrong options.

Common mistakes: Inferred, not measured. Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

How each command word is answered

Command wordTimes askedWhat to write
Calculate2Write price - (sum of unit costs) or TR - TC, then the profit or loss with currency.
multiple-choice3Work out TR first, then take away TC; reject options that skip the subtraction or call a profit a loss.

Worked explanation

  1. Cost per pair = raw materials + labour = $17 + $35 = $52.
  2. Profit per pair = selling price - cost per pair = $89 - $52 = $37.
  3. The answer is positive, so it is a profit.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Profit per pair = $89 - ($17 + $35) = $89 - $52 = $37 profit on each pair of shoes.

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for the line $89 - ($17 + $35). One for the profit of $37 with the dollar sign. The correct figure alone earns both marks.

Calculation check

  • 17+35 = 52
  • 89-(17+35) = 37
