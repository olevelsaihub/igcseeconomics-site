Knowledge card

Profit or loss from revenue and costs

Calculation

Profit = total revenue − total cost. Per unit, profit = price − all the costs per unit. A negative answer is a loss.

The calculation

Profit = Total revenue − Total cost

Total revenue = price × quantity sold.

To find TR:TR = Profit + TC

To find TC:TC = TR − Profit

  • Add every unit cost first.
  • Negative means a loss.
  • Give the $ sign.

Try itA mug sells for $15. Clay costs $4 and labour $7 per mug. Find the profit per mug.

  1. Cost = 4 + 7 = $11.
  2. 15 − 11 = $4.
  3. $4 profit per mug.

The method

  1. Revenue

    Price × quantity, or just the price per unit.

  2. Matching cost

    Total cost, or the sum of all unit costs.

  3. Subtract

    Revenue − cost, with the working.

  4. Label it

    Give $ and say profit or loss.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Revenue as profit

    Always take the costs off.

  • ✗
    One cost only

    Add every cost per unit.

  • ✗
    Wrong label

    Positive is profit; negative is loss.

Worked explanation

  1. Cost per pair = raw materials + labour = $17 + $35 = $52.
  2. Profit per pair = selling price - cost per pair = $89 - $52 = $37.
  3. The answer is positive, so it is a profit.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Profit per pair = $89 - ($17 + $35) = $89 - $52 = $37 profit on each pair of shoes.

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for the line $89 - ($17 + $35). One for the profit of $37 with the dollar sign. The correct figure alone earns both marks.

Calculation check

  • 17+35 = 52
  • 89-(17+35) = 37
