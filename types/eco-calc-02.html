Excess demand and excess supply

Calculation

Excess demand (a shortage) is Qd − Qs when demand is bigger. Excess supply (a surplus) is Qs − Qd when supply is bigger. Answer in units of the good.

2

The calculation

Excess demand = Qd − Qs

If Qs is bigger: excess supply = Qs − Qd.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find Qd:Qd = Qs + excess demand

To find Qs:Qs = Qd − excess demand

  • Add every period of demand first.
  • Larger minus smaller: never negative.
  • Answer in units, not $.

Try itSeats demanded on three nights: 420, 380, 510. Seats supplied in total: 1 200. Find the gap.

  1. Qd = 420 + 380 + 510 = 1 310.
  2. 1 310 − 1 200 = 110.
  3. 110 seats of excess demand.

5

The method

  1. Total demand

    Read it at the price, or add all the periods.

  2. Matching supply

    Use supply for the same period.

  3. Subtract

    Larger minus smaller, with the working.

  4. Name it

    Say excess demand or supply, with the unit.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    One period only

    Add every month or day before comparing.

  • ✗
    Negative answer

    Take the smaller from the larger.

  • ✗
    D and S mixed up

    Read Qd from the demand curve.

  • ✗
    Adding slips

    Check the total of big numbers twice.

What a complete response includes

Worked explanation

  1. Add the tickets demanded for the three concerts: 4,200 + 6,000 + 3,250 = 13,450.
  2. The tickets supplied for all three concerts total 9,600.
  3. Demand is bigger than supply, so there is excess demand.
  4. Excess demand = 13,450 - 9,600 = 3,850 tickets.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Total quantity demanded = 4,200 + 6,000 + 3,250 = 13,450 tickets. Excess demand = 13,450 - 9,600 = 3,850 tickets.

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One is for the working that adds the three demand figures and takes away the 9,600 supplied. The other is for the correct excess demand of 3,850 tickets. The right answer on its own also scores both.

Calculation check

  • 4200+6000+3250 = 13450
  • (4200+6000+3250)-9600 = 3850
