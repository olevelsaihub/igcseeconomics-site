GDP per capita

Calculation

GDP per capita = GDP ÷ population: the average output per person. Put both numbers in the same units before you divide.

The calculation

GDP per capita = GDP ÷ population

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find GDP:GDP = per capita × population

  • 1 billion = 1,000 million.
  • Divide GDP by population, not the other way.

Try itGDP is $8.4 billion; population is 2.4 million. Find GDP per capita.

  1. $8.4 billion = $8,400 million
  2. 8,400 ÷ 2.4
  3. $3,500 per person

5

The method

  1. Check units

    Make GDP and population both millions (or both billions).

  2. Divide

    Write GDP ÷ population with the numbers.

  3. Finish

    Round as asked and give $ per person.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Units mixed

    Billions over millions is 1,000 times out.

  • ✗
    Upside down

    Population ÷ GDP is wrong.

  • ✗
    No $

    Show the currency.

Worked explanation

  1. GDP = US$202,600 million; population = 92.7 million. Both are in millions, so the millions cancel.
  2. GDP per capita = 202,600 / 92.7 = 2,185.544...
  3. Round to two decimal places: $2,185.54.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

GDP per capita = GDP / population = $202,600 million / 92.7 million = $2,185.54 per person (to 2 d.p.).

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for 202,600 million divided by 92.7 million. One for $2,185.54. The right answer with no working also earns both marks.
