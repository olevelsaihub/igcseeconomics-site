Knowledge card

Average cost per unit

Calculation

Average cost is the cost of making one unit. Add fixed and variable cost to get total cost, then divide by the number of units made.

The calculation

Average cost = Total cost ÷ Quantity

Total cost = fixed cost + variable cost

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find TC:TC = AC × Q

  • Use total cost, not one part.
  • Answer per unit, with $.

Try itFixed cost $900, variable cost $1,500, output 400 units. Find average cost.

  1. TC = $900 + $1,500 = $2,400.
  2. AC = $2,400 ÷ 400 = $6.00 per unit.

5

The method

  1. Add the costs

    Fixed + variable = total cost.

  2. Divide

    Total cost ÷ units made.

  3. Write it out

    Show the working; answer in $ to two decimal places.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    One cost only

    Divide total cost, not just fixed or variable.

  • ✗
    Total, not average

    Divide by output before you stop.

  • ✗
    No $ sign

    Average cost is money per unit.

Worked explanation

Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Average cost = total cost / quantity = ($6,200 + $3,550) / 3,000 = $9,750 / 3,000 = $3.25 per unit.

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for ($6,200 + $3,550) / 3,000 written out. One for the answer $3.25 with the dollar sign. The right answer alone earns both.
