Knowledge card

Wage income from an hourly rate

Calculation

A wage is the pay for labour, often set per hour. Gross income = hourly wage × hours worked, given with the currency to two decimal places.

Meaning

Why
Monthly, so use the month's hours.

5

The method

  1. Find the rate

    Pick out the hourly wage.

  2. Find the hours

    Use the hours for the week or month asked.

  3. Multiply

    Write rate × hours with the numbers.

  4. Finish

    Currency sign, two decimal places.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Rate only

    The hourly wage is not the income.

  • ✗
    Decimal slips

    Check the size: about $8 × 30 is about $240.

  • ✗
    No currency

    Always write $, € or £.

Worked explanation

  1. The minimum wage is €5.87 per hour and the worker works 175 hours a month.
  2. Gross income = €5.87 x 175 = €1,027.25.
