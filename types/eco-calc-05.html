Knowledge card

Calculating income elasticity of demand (YED)

Calculation
  • Income elasticity of demand (YED) measures how much quantity demanded responds to a change in income.
  • Formula: YED = % change in quantity demanded / % change in income.
  • YED above 1: a luxury good (demand grows faster than income).
  • YED between 0 and 1: a normal good that is a necessity.
  • YED below 0 (negative): an inferior good; people buy less as income rises.

Before you begin

  • Meaning of demand and income
  • Normal and inferior goods
  • Dividing and rounding decimals
1

The method

  1. Write the % change in quantity demanded and the % change in income, with signs.

    Why: The sign tells you whether the good is normal or inferior.

  2. Divide quantity change by income change.

    Why: Income is the cause, so it goes on the bottom.

  3. Round to the decimal places asked; do not add a % sign.

    Why: YED is a plain number.

  4. If asked to classify, use the value: above 1 luxury, 0 to 1 necessity, below 0 inferior.

    Why: Multiple-choice versions test the class, not the number.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Putting income on top of the fraction.
  • ✗
    Calling a good with YED between 0 and 1 a luxury.
  • ✗
    Confusing 'unitary' (exactly 1) with 'luxury' (above 1) in multiple choice.
  • ✗
    Not rounding to two decimal places when asked.

Common mistakes: Inferred, not measured. Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

Worked explanation

  1. Income rose by 7% and quantity demanded rose by 11%.
  2. YED = 11 / 7 = 1.5714...
  3. Round to two decimal places: 1.57.
  4. As 1.57 is above 1, demand rises faster than income, so this is a luxury good.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

YED = % change in quantity demanded / % change in income = 11% / 7% = 1.5714 = 1.57 (to 2 d.p.).

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for showing 11 divided by 7, and one for the result 1.57 to two decimal places. A correct answer with no working still gets both.

  • round(11 ÷ 7, 2) = 1.57
