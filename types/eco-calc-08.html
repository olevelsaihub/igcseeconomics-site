Knowledge card

Sorting costs into fixed and variable

Calculation
  • Fixed costs stay the same whatever the output: rent, advertising, insurance, buying a machine.
  • Variable costs rise and fall with output: raw materials, labour paid by output, electricity per hour of production.
  • Total fixed cost = the sum of all fixed items only.
  • Total variable cost = the sum of all variable items only (multiply any per-unit or per-hour cost by the quantity first).

Before you begin

  • Meaning of fixed and variable costs
  • Adding numbers with currency
1

The method

  1. Ask of each item: would this cost change if the firm made more?

    Why: This is the test that separates fixed from variable.

  2. Keep only the group the question asks for.

    Why: Adding every item gives total cost, not the answer.

  3. Multiply any per-hour or per-unit item by the quantity.

    Why: A rate is not yet a total.

  4. Add the chosen items, write the sum, and give the total with currency.

    Why: The sum shows your selection, which earns the first mark.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Treating labour as fixed when the table says it is paid according to output.
  • ✗
    Counting advertising or insurance as variable.
  • ✗
    Adding every item in the table.
  • ✗
    Including a one-off purchase price as a variable cost.

Common mistakes: Inferred, not measured. Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

Worked explanation

  1. Rent ($25,000) and insurance ($5,500) stay the same whatever is made, so they are fixed.
  2. Raw materials ($12,000) and labour paid by output ($115,000) change with output, so they are variable.
  3. Total variable costs = $12,000 + $115,000 = $127,000.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Variable costs are raw materials and labour (because labour pay depends on output). Total variable costs = $12,000 + $115,000 = $127,000 per month.

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for adding the right two items, $12,000 + $115,000. One for the total, $127,000, with the dollar sign. The right total alone scores both.

Calculation check

  • 12000+115000 = 127000
