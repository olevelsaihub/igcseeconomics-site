Pearson Edexcel · International GCSE Economics · 4EC1

Percentage change, including inflation from a price index

(new - old) / old x 100 applied to any series: GDP, GDP per capita, population, profit, interest rate, spending, deficit, sales, and a consumer price index (including the base-100 shortcut and scaling a basket price by the index).

★★☆☆☆ · 15 of 40 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

Two values of the same thing at two dates (GDP, profit, population, CPI) and a request for the percentage change, rate of inflation or new basket price.

Knowledge card

Percentage change, including inflation from a price index

Calculation

Percentage change compares the change with where you started: (new − old) ÷ old × 100. Inflation is the percentage change in a price index such as the CPI.

Find the inflation rate since the base year, then for 2021 to 2022.

  1. Base year = 100
  2. Since base: 10%
  3. 2021 to 2022: 5.77%

Base year = 100

Shortcut
From a base of 100, inflation = index − 100.

Since base: 10%

Working
110 − 100 = 10%

2021 to 2022: 5.77%

Working
(110 − 104) ÷ 104 × 100 = 5.769…
Answer
5.77% to 2 d.p.

The calculation

% change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find % change:(new − old) ÷ old × 100

To find new price:new price = old price × new index ÷ old index

  • Divide by the old value.
  • Add the % sign.
  • A fall keeps its minus sign.

Try itA town's population falls from 2 500 to 2 300. Find the % change.

  1. Change = 2 300 − 2 500 = −200
  2. −200 ÷ 2 500 × 100 = −8%

5

The method

  1. Pick two values

    Use the two dates asked; ignore years in between.

  2. Find the change

    New − old, keeping the sign.

  3. Divide by old, × 100

    Write the full expression.

  4. Index shortcuts

    Base 100: index − 100. Basket: old price × new index ÷ old index.

  5. Finish

    Round as asked and add %.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Dividing by new

    Always divide by the earlier value.

  • ✗
    Middle year used

    Read the dates in the question.

  • ✗
    Lost minus sign

    A fall is a negative change.

What a complete response includes

One mark for change over original value times 100 with the right figures; one mark for the correctly rounded percentage with the % sign. The number without % usually earns one mark only.

Worked explanation

  1. The question asks about 2017 to 2019, so ignore 2018.
  2. Change in profit = $87,100 - $80,750 = $6,350.
  3. Percentage change = 6,350 / 80,750 x 100 = 7.8637...%.
  4. Round to two decimal places: 7.86%.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Percentage change = (87,100 - 80,750) / 80,750 x 100 = 6,350 / 80,750 x 100 = 7.86% (to 2 d.p.).

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for the change divided by the 2017 profit, times 100. One for 7.86% with the % sign and rounded to two decimal places. The right answer alone earns both.
