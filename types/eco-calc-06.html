Knowledge card

Total revenue: price times quantity

Calculation
  • Total revenue (TR) is all the money a firm receives from selling its output.
  • Formula: TR = price (P) x quantity sold (Q).
  • Rearranged: Q = TR / P, and P = TR / Q.
  • If sales are split over several weeks, add the quantities first, then multiply by the price.
  • Costs are not part of revenue; cost figures in the question are there to test you.

Before you begin

  • Difference between price and cost
  • Multiplying larger numbers and decimals
1

The method

  1. Find the selling price per unit and the quantity sold (add the quantities if they are given by week or product).

    Why: TR uses total units sold at the selling price.

  2. Ignore cost per unit or average cost figures.

    Why: Revenue comes only from sales, not costs.

  3. Multiply P x Q, or divide TR by P if the quantity is unknown, and write the calculation.

    Why: Showing it earns the working mark.

  4. Give the answer with the currency sign (or in units if you found quantity).

    Why: A number without its currency may lose the answer mark.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Multiplying quantity by cost per unit instead of by price.
  • ✗
    Leaving out the currency sign on the final answer.
  • ✗
    Forgetting to add all weekly sales before multiplying.
  • ✗
    Choosing the wrong product when told to use the one with elastic or inelastic PED.

Common mistakes: Inferred, not measured. Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

What a complete response includes

Working mark for price times quantity with the right figures (or the right product chosen); answer mark for the revenue with currency. A number missing its currency often gets only one mark.

Worked explanation

  1. The selling price is €75 per unit and monthly sales are 250 units.
  2. The cost per unit of €35 is not needed for revenue.
  3. TR = €75 x 250 = €18,750.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Total revenue = price x quantity = €75 x 250 = €18,750.

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for writing €75 x 250, and one for €18,750 with the euro sign. The correct answer alone earns both; 18,750 with no euro sign would earn one.

Calculation check

  • 75 × 250 = 18750
