Knowledge card

Total revenue: price times quantity

Calculation

Total revenue is all the money a firm receives from sales: price × quantity sold. Costs are never part of revenue.

2

The calculation

Total revenue = price × quantity sold

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find TR:TR = P × Q

To find Q:Q = TR ÷ P

To find P:P = TR ÷ Q

  • Use the price, not the cost.
  • Add the $ sign to a revenue answer.

Try itA stall sells 75 cakes at $3 each. Find total revenue.

  1. TR = P × Q
  2. = $3 × 75
  3. Answer: $225

5

The method

  1. Find P and Q

    Add weekly sales first if Q is split.

  2. Ignore costs

    Revenue comes only from sales.

  3. Write the sum

    P × Q, or TR ÷ P for quantity.

  4. Add the unit

    $ for revenue, units for quantity.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Cost × quantity

    Use the selling price.

  • ✗
    One week only

    Add all the weeks before multiplying.

  • ✗
    No $ sign

    A bare number can lose the mark.

Worked explanation

  1. The selling price is €75 per unit and monthly sales are 250 units.
  2. The cost per unit of €35 is not needed for revenue.
  3. TR = €75 x 250 = €18,750.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Total revenue = price x quantity = €75 x 250 = €18,750.

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for writing €75 x 250, and one for €18,750 with the euro sign. The correct answer alone earns both; 18,750 with no euro sign would earn one.
