Knowledge card

Fiscal balance: budget surplus or deficit

Calculation

Fiscal balance = government revenue − government spending. A minus answer is a deficit; a plus answer is a surplus.

The method

  1. Take revenue

    Write it first.

  2. Subtract spending

    Show the subtraction.

  3. Read the sign

    − deficit, + surplus.

  4. Add units

    Same as the table, e.g. $bn.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Spending − revenue

    This flips the sign.

  • ✗
    Adding

    Balance is a difference.

  • ✗
    No units

    The answer needs them.

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Revenue = Rs 2,080,201 crore; expenditure = Rs 2,784,200 crore.
  2. Fiscal balance = 2,080,201 - 2,784,200 = -703,999.
  3. The answer is negative, so India had a fiscal deficit.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Fiscal balance = revenue - expenditure = Rs 2,080,201 crore - Rs 2,784,200 crore = -Rs 703,999 crore, a fiscal deficit.

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for revenue minus spending with the figures. One for -703,999 Rs crore with the minus sign and currency. The right answer alone also earns both.
