Knowledge card

Calculating price elasticity of demand (PED)

Calculation
  • Price elasticity of demand (PED) measures how much quantity demanded responds to a change in price.
  • Formula: PED = % change in quantity demanded / % change in price.
  • A price rise is a positive change; a price fall is a negative change. The same rule applies to quantity.
  • PED is normally negative, because price and quantity demanded move in opposite directions.
  • Ignoring the minus sign: above 1 is price elastic, below 1 is price inelastic, exactly 1 is unitary.
  • Rearranged: % change in quantity demanded = PED x % change in price.

Before you begin

  • Meaning of demand
  • Positive and negative numbers
  • Rounding to two decimal places
1

The method

  1. Write the two percentage changes with signs: + for a rise, - for a fall.

    Why: The sign of PED depends on these signs.

  2. Put quantity on top and price on the bottom: % change in Qd / % change in P.

    Why: Putting them upside down is the most costly slip.

  3. Divide, or if PED is given, multiply PED by the price change to find the quantity change.

    Why: The rearranged form answers the 'find the change' version.

  4. Round to the decimal places asked and keep the minus sign (add % when the answer is a percentage change).

    Why: The answer mark needs the correct value, sign and precision.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Dividing price change by quantity change (formula upside down).
  • ✗
    Dropping the minus sign, which loses the answer mark.
  • ✗
    Rounding wrongly or not to two decimal places when asked.
  • ✗
    Adding a % sign to the PED value, which is a plain number.

Common mistakes: Inferred, not measured. Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

What a complete response includes

One mark for the quantity change over the price change written with numbers; one mark for the final value with the minus sign and the requested rounding. Working without the minus sign keeps only the first mark.

Worked explanation

  1. Price increased by 1.6%, so the price change is +1.6.
  2. Quantity demanded fell by 2.1%, so the quantity change is -2.1.
  3. PED = -2.1 / 1.6 = -1.3125.
  4. Round to two decimal places: -1.31. Ignoring the sign it is above 1, so demand is price elastic.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

PED = % change in quantity demanded / % change in price = -2.1% / 1.6% = -1.3125 = -1.31 (to 2 d.p.).

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One mark for writing -2.1 over 1.6. The second mark is for -1.31, with the minus sign and rounded to two decimal places. The correct answer alone scores both.

Calculation check

  • -2.1 ÷ 1.6 = -1.3125
  • round(-2.1 ÷ 1.6, 2) = -1.31
