Knowledge card

Social costs and social benefits formulas

Written

Social costs = private costs + external costs. Social benefits = private benefits + external benefits. Each is the total for the whole of society.

4

The method

  1. Costs or benefits?

    Read the question word carefully.

  2. Private part first

    Private costs or private benefits.

  3. Add the external part

    Plus external costs or external benefits.

  4. Meaning? Say total

    Name both parts: the total for society.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Mixing the two

    Costs with costs, benefits with benefits.

  • ✗
    Subtracting

    Always add the external part.

  • ✗
    Half a formula

    Include the private part.

Worked explanation

  1. The question asks about costs, not benefits.
  2. Private part: private costs paid by the producer or consumer.
  3. External part: external costs on third parties.
  4. Add them and say this is the total cost to society.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Social costs are the total costs of an activity to the whole of society. They are the private costs paid by the producer or consumer making the decision, plus the external costs that fall on third parties: social costs = private costs + external costs.

How the marks are earned

1 mark for private costs. 1 mark for adding external costs.
