Knowledge card

Trade and current account balances

Calculation
  • Exports are goods and services sold abroad; imports are goods and services bought from abroad.
  • Visible trade is trade in goods; invisible trade is trade in services.
  • Visible trade balance = exports of goods - imports of goods.
  • Invisible trade balance = exports of services - imports of services.
  • In these questions the current account balance is worked out as visible balance + invisible balance (total exports - total imports). The full current account also includes income flows and transfers, but the questions give only trade figures.
  • Negative = deficit; positive = surplus.

Before you begin

  • Exports and imports
  • Goods versus services
  • Negative numbers
1

The method

  1. Decide which flows are needed: goods only (visible), services only (invisible), or all (current account).

    Why: Using the wrong rows is the main trap.

  2. Calculate exports - imports, or add the component balances; write it out.

    Why: Earns the working mark.

  3. Keep the sign: negative is a deficit, positive a surplus.

    Why: The sign shows the position.

  4. Give the answer with currency and units.

    Why: The answer mark needs them.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Using services when the question asks for visible (goods) trade, or the reverse.
  • ✗
    Calculating imports - exports, which reverses the sign.
  • ✗
    Adding exports and imports together.
  • ✗
    Leaving off the currency and units.

Common mistakes: Inferred, not measured. Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

How each command word is answered

Command wordTimes askedWhat to write
Calculate5Write exports - imports for the right flows, then the signed balance with units.
multiple-choice2Check whether the option needs goods only, services only or both; wrong options mix them or reverse the subtraction.

Worked explanation

  1. Visible trade means goods only.
  2. Exports of goods = €150bn; imports of goods = €120bn.
  3. Visible trade balance = €150bn - €120bn = €30bn, a surplus.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Visible trade balance = exports of goods - imports of goods = €150bn - €120bn = €30bn (a visible trade surplus).

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for €150bn - €120bn. One for €30bn with the currency and units. The correct answer alone earns both.
