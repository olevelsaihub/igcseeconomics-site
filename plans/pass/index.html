Why these types are in the plan
49selected question types
7learning days

The review covered 40 current component papers from 2019–2025.

Current-paper marks were assigned to each part’s sole primary type, and every selected type’s prerequisites were added.

Historical coverage check

20 of 20 current paper pairs cleared the target

The route covered at least 96/160 primary-owned marks on every complete 2019–2025 pair. Each pair stayed at least 10 marks above the higher of its own historical Grade 5 boundary and the matching June 2026 Grade 5 boundary.

This is 100% historical paper-pair coverage, not a 100% probability claim for a future student grade. Accuracy, timing, independent recall and unseen wording still matter.

Completion time

7 learning days plus 6 paper sessions

Study 7 types per day, then sit three complete Paper 1 + Paper 2 mock pairs as six separate paper sessions. That is 13 active days.

This keeps the olevels.com Pass Plan pace of about 6.6 types per learning day.

Readiness gate

Completion is practice. Fresh papers confirm readiness.

After completing the plan, attempt three fresh, unseen 160-mark Paper 1 + Paper 2 paper pairs under timed conditions. Have each pair independently marked with its official scheme. Grade 5 readiness is supported only when every score is at least 10 marks above that exact pair’s published Grade 5 boundary.

Supporting skills for Economics

Use these short bridges when a starting skill needs strengthening.

Reading economic data and extracts

Read a short extract, table or chart and pick out the figures, trends and facts a question needs

Answering command words

Use the technique cards for multiple choice, Define, State, Describe, Calculate, Draw, Explain, Analyse, Assess and Evaluate

Percentages, rounding and money

Work with percentages, percentage change, decimals, rounding to two places, millions and billions, and currency units with a calculator

Drawing and reading supply and demand diagrams

Draw and label axes, curves and equilibrium points, read values off a graph, and show a shift or movement with arrows
