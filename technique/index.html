Economics / Answer technique cards

Pearson Edexcel · International GCSE Economics · 4EC1

Answer technique cards

How each command word is marked, with a method and an authored worked illustration.

01

Analyse: build chains of reasoning from the data (6 marks)

Pearson's meaning: write an extended answer that expands and explores one economic idea or issue, set in the context of the question. In 4EC1 it always starts 'With reference to the data above and your knowledge of economics, analyse...'. There is no judgement to make: you explain how and why something happens, step by step, using the case.

02

Assess: weigh up factors using the data and judge (9 marks)

Pearson's meaning: write a longer answer that uses the information you are given to weigh the factors against each other in an economic setting. You must show both sides (why it works or helps, and why it may not) and say which side is stronger in this case.

03

Pearson's meaning: use maths skills to reach an answer from the given data. Calculators may be used and working should be shown. Common 4EC1 calculations: percentages, percentage change, totals such as total revenue, trade balances, currency conversion and opportunity cost from a PPC.

04

Define and What is meant by: give precise definitions (1-2 marks)

Define: give the meaning of a term from the specification (usually 1 mark). What is meant by: define the term, and the definition must have two separate parts (2 marks). No example or context is needed.

05

Describe: name one consequence and develop it (2 marks)

Pearson's meaning: identify a consequence of an economic event and briefly develop it. Questions read 'Describe one advantage / impact / benefit / policy ...'. Only one point is needed.

06

Draw: show the shift and label the new equilibrium (3 marks)

Pearson's meaning: draw a diagram with two or three separate points that earn marks. In 4EC1 you are given a supply and demand diagram (a product, labour or currency market) and asked to draw the effect of a change, labelling the new curve, the new equilibrium price (or wage or exchange rate) and the new quantity.

07

Evaluate: balanced argument and a supported conclusion (12 marks)

Pearson's meaning: write a longer answer that applies what you know from the specification and ends with a conclusion backed by reasons about the economic situation in the case. It is the last part of each 4EC1 paper and always uses the case data. You must argue both sides and finish with a clear, reasoned judgement.

08

Explain: one point, two linked expansions, in context (3 marks)

Pearson's meaning: give a statement of fact, then two further expansion points. The expansions may build on each other or both come from the same fact. The question places the answer in context (a country, industry or group), and your answer must use it.

09

Multiple choice: choose the one correct answer (1 mark)

Pearson's meaning: pick the right option from those offered. These test recall of specification content, or need a short calculation to reach the answer. In 4EC1 there are four options (A to D) and one is correct.

10

State or identify: give one correct point (1 mark)

Pearson's meaning of State: a short answer of one sentence at most, about a piece of information from the specification. Identify works the same way in 4EC1: name one thing. No explanation is needed.
