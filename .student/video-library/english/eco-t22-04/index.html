Think of trainers made abroad. If they cross the border with no import tax, they reach your local shop more cheaply, and you have more styles to choose from. Free trade means buying and selling between countries with no tariffs, no quotas and no other restrictions. A tariff is a tax on imports. A quota is a limit on imports. Removing a tariff cuts the cost of supplying imports. So supply shifts to the right. The price falls and the quantity bought rises.

Step one. Find the group in the question: consumers, domestic firms, exporters or the government. Then check if it asks for gains or losses. Step two. Know the gains and losses. Consumers gain lower prices and more choice. Exporters gain a wider market and cheaper inputs. Firms facing imports may lose sales and jobs. The government loses tariff revenue. Step three. Explain the chain. No tariff means a lower cost, which means a lower price, which means more sales. Then use the case. Trade patterns. Developed countries tend to export manufactured goods and services, and import primary products. Developing countries tend to export primary products, such as crops and minerals.

Here is a real question from June 2023. The R C E P is the world's largest free trade agreement, with fifteen members and nearly thirty per cent of world G D P. Analyse the benefits of free trade for firms inside it. Six marks. Use the data. Nearly thirty per cent of world G D P sits inside the bloc. That is a huge market with no tariffs. Chain one. With no tariffs inside the bloc, a firm's exports are cheaper in the fourteen other members. So it wins customers and its revenue rises. Chain two. Selling in fifteen countries spreads risk. If demand falls at home, the firm can still sell to the other members. Chain three. Firms can import cheaper raw materials from members with no tariff. Costs fall, so they can cut prices or earn more profit. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

A common mistake is saying free trade means trade that costs nothing. Goods still cost money. Free means free of barriers. Another is writing about consumers when the question asks about domestic firms. Stay with the named group.

Now you try. Country Z removes its twenty per cent tariff on imported bicycles. The figure is just an example. Explain one disadvantage for Country Z's own bicycle makers. Pause here and work it out.

Without the tariff, imported bicycles cost less to supply. Supply shifts right, and the price of imported bicycles falls. So buyers switch from local bicycles to cheaper imports. Country Z's makers lose sales and profit, and some workers may lose their jobs. Remember: find the group and the side, build the chain from no barriers to prices and sales, and use the case.
