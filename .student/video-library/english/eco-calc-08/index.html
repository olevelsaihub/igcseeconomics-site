Think of a pizza shop. Its rent is the same whether it makes ten pizzas or a thousand. But every extra pizza needs more cheese. Here is the pattern. Rent stays at two thousand dollars. Cheese at one dollar fifty a pizza rises as output rises. Fixed costs include rent, insurance, advertising and buying a machine. Variable costs include raw materials, labour paid by output, and electricity used per hour of production.

Step one. Take each cost and ask: would this change if the firm made more? Yes means variable. No means fixed. Steps two and three. Keep only the group asked for. Multiply any per-hour or per-unit cost by the quantity first. Four dollars an hour for thirty hours is one hundred and twenty dollars. Step four. Add the chosen items, write the sum, and give the total with currency.

Here is a real question from June 2022. A clothing firm's monthly costs are rent, raw materials, insurance, and labour paid according to output. Calculate total variable costs. Two marks. Rent and insurance stay the same whatever is made: fixed. Raw materials rise with output. Labour is paid according to output, so it is variable too. Add the two variable costs. Twelve thousand plus one hundred and fifteen thousand is one hundred and twenty-seven thousand dollars. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

A common mistake is treating labour as fixed. When pay depends on output, labour is a variable cost. Another is adding every item in the table. Add only the group the question asks for.

Now you try. A furniture maker pays rent of three thousand dollars, wood of four thousand two hundred dollars, and advertising of six hundred dollars a month. A machine costs five dollars an hour to run, for one hundred and sixty hours. Calculate total fixed and total variable costs. Pause here and work it out.

Sort them. Rent and advertising are fixed. Wood and machine running are variable. The machine costs five times one hundred and sixty, which is eight hundred dollars. Total fixed costs are three thousand six hundred dollars. Total variable costs are five thousand dollars. Here are the two totals side by side. Remember: ask if each cost changes with output, multiply any rates, then add the right group. Now try the practice questions.
