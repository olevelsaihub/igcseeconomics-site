Economics / Describe: name one consequence and develop it (2 marks)

Pearson Edexcel · International GCSE Economics · 4EC1

Describe: name one consequence and develop it (2 marks)

Pearson's meaning: identify a consequence of an economic event and briefly develop it. Questions read 'Describe one advantage / impact / benefit / policy ...'. Only one point is needed.

Command word: DescribeUsually 2 marksAbout 2 minutes

What Pearson asks

Pearson's meaning: identify a consequence of an economic event and briefly develop it. Questions read 'Describe one advantage / impact / benefit / policy ...'. Only one point is needed.

How the marks are earned

Two AO1 marks: 1 for naming a relevant point (the advantage, impact, benefit or policy) and 1 for developing it with a short reason or result. A second, different point earns nothing extra.

Method

  1. Name ONE point in a few words: 'One advantage is ...'.

    Why: This earns the first mark.

  2. Add one development: 'because ...' or 'so ...' that explains why or what happens next.

    Why: This earns the second mark.

  3. Keep it to one or two sentences and do not give a second point.

    Why: Only one point is marked; extra points waste time.

Answer frame

One (advantage / impact / benefit) is ... . This is because / so ... .

Common errors

Inferred, not measured: Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

  • Naming the point but not developing it (1 mark only).
  • Giving two or three different points instead of developing one.
  • Development that just repeats the point in other words.
  • Answering for the wrong group, for example firms when the question asks about consumers.

Worked illustration

November 2025 · Paper 2 · Q1(d)

The complete original question is shown; the illustration answers part (d).

Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

One advantage is that consumers may get better quality goods and services. A private firm wants to make a profit, so it has a strong reason to improve its service to win customers and stop them going to rivals.

Why it earns the marks

1 mark for naming the advantage (better quality) and 1 mark for developing it (the profit motive pushes the firm to improve to keep customers).
