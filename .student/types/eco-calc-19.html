Knowledge card

Applying a percentage: interest, tax and tariffs

Calculation
  • Percentage of an amount = rate / 100 x amount.
  • Interest is the reward for saving: interest = rate / 100 x savings.
  • Income tax is charged only on the part of income in each taxed band (taxable income).
  • Net pay = gross pay - income tax.
  • A tariff is a tax on imports: price with tariff = import price + tariff.

Before you begin

  • Percentages
  • Meaning of interest, income tax and tariffs
1

The method

  1. Find the base the rate applies to: savings, the taxed part of income only, or the import price.

    Why: Applying tax to all income is the common trap.

  2. Work out the amount: rate / 100 x base.

    Why: This is the interest, tax or tariff.

  3. If a final figure is wanted, subtract (tax from pay) or add (interest or tariff).

    Why: Net pay and final price need this step.

  4. Write the working and give the answer with currency.

    Why: Earns both marks.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Taxing the whole salary instead of only the taxed band.
  • ✗
    Giving the tax or interest when the question asks for net pay or the final total.
  • ✗
    Using rate x amount without dividing by 100.
  • ✗
    Leaving out the currency sign.

Common mistakes: Inferred, not measured. Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

Worked explanation

  1. George earns €21,000 but only €1,500 is taxed, at 20%.
  2. Tax = 20 / 100 x €1,500 = €300.
  3. Net pay = €21,000 - €300 = €20,700.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Tax = 20% x €1,500 = €300. Net pay = €21,000 - €300 = €20,700.

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for showing €21,000 minus 20% of €1,500. One for €20,700 with the euro sign. The correct answer with no working earns both.

Calculation check

  • 20 ÷ 100 × 1500 = 300
  • 21000-20 ÷ 100 × 1500 = 20700
