Applying a percentage: interest, tax and tariffs

Finding a given percentage of a sum and, where asked, adding it (interest earned, tariff-inclusive price) or subtracting it (net pay after tax).

Calculation

Interest, income tax and tariffs are all rate ÷ 100 × base. Find the right base first, then add or subtract if the question wants a final figure.

The calculation

Amount = rate ÷ 100 × base

Then subtract (tax from pay) or add (interest, tariff) if a final figure is asked.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

  • Tax only the taxed part of income.
  • Divide the rate by 100.

Try itGross pay $18,000; only $4,000 is taxed, at 25%. Find net pay.

  1. Tax = 25 ÷ 100 × $4,000 = $1,000.
  2. Net pay = $18,000 − $1,000 = $17,000.

5

The method

  1. Find the base

    Savings, the taxed part of pay, or the import price.

  2. Apply the rate

    rate ÷ 100 × base.

  3. Finish

    Subtract tax from pay; add interest or a tariff.

  4. Show it

    Write the working; answer with $.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Whole salary taxed

    Tax only the band that is taxed.

  • ✗
    Stopping early

    Net pay or a final price needs the last step.

  • ✗
    No ÷ 100

    20% of $5,000 is $1,000, not $100,000.

Worked explanation

  1. George earns €21,000 but only €1,500 is taxed, at 20%.
  2. Tax = 20 / 100 x €1,500 = €300.
  3. Net pay = €21,000 - €300 = €20,700.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Tax = 20% x €1,500 = €300. Net pay = €21,000 - €300 = €20,700.

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for showing €21,000 minus 20% of €1,500. One for €20,700 with the euro sign. The correct answer with no working earns both.
