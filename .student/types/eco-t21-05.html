Label the economic cycle diagram

Written

The economic cycle shows real GDP rising and falling around a trend line. The phases always run in order: boom, downturn, recession, recovery.

Name the phase in each of years 2 to 5.

  1. Boom
  2. Downturn
  3. Recession
  4. Recovery

Why

Downturn

Why
Just after the peak, GDP starts to fall.

Why
98 is the trough: output at its lowest.

Why

Why

Why

Does it score?

Exam question

A cycle diagram shows Boom at the peak. Label the other three phases. (3 marks)

Decide first: would this answer earn the mark? Then tap it to check.

Tap an answer to see if it earns the mark.

4

The method

  1. Find the given label

    Note where it sits: peak, falling, trough or rising.

  2. Anchor two

    Boom at a peak, recession at a trough.

  3. Fill between

    Downturn on the fall, recovery on the rise.

  4. Check the order

    Boom, downturn, recession, recovery. None repeated.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Swapped pair

    Recovery is never on a falling stretch.

  • ✗
    Trough as downturn

    The lowest point is recession.

  • ✗
    Repeating the given

    Write only the missing three.

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. The diagram shows real GDP against time, a trend line, and a wavy GDP line; the peak box already says Boom.
  2. Move right from the peak: the line starts to fall, so that box is Downturn.
  3. The lowest point of the wave is the trough: label it Recession.
  4. After the trough the line climbs back up: label it Recovery.
  5. Check the order reads boom, downturn, recession, recovery.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Box on the falling section after the peak: Downturn. Box at the lowest point (trough) of the wave: Recession. Box on the rising section after the trough: Recovery. (Boom was already shown at the peak.)

How the marks are earned

3 marks (AO2): one each for Downturn, Recession and Recovery written in the correct boxes. Recession may also be called slump or depression.
