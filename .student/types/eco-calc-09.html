Knowledge card

Average cost per unit

Calculation
  • Average cost (AC), also called average total cost, is the cost of making one unit.
  • Formula: AC = total cost / quantity produced.
  • Total cost = total fixed cost + total variable cost.
  • Average cost is money per unit, usually given to two decimal places (cents).

Before you begin

  • Fixed and variable costs
  • Total cost formula
  • Dividing to two decimal places
1

The method

  1. Add total fixed cost and total variable cost to get total cost.

    Why: AC uses all costs, not just one part.

  2. Divide total cost by the number of units.

    Why: This spreads the cost over each unit.

  3. Write the working, then give the answer in currency to two decimal places.

    Why: The answer mark needs the per-unit value with currency.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Dividing only fixed or only variable cost by output.
  • ✗
    Giving total cost instead of the cost per unit.
  • ✗
    Leaving out the currency sign.

Common mistakes: Inferred, not measured. Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Average cost = total cost / quantity = ($6,200 + $3,550) / 3,000 = $9,750 / 3,000 = $3.25 per unit.

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for ($6,200 + $3,550) / 3,000 written out. One for the answer $3.25 with the dollar sign. The right answer alone earns both.
