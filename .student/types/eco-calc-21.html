Knowledge card

Fiscal balance: budget surplus or deficit

Calculation
  • Fiscal balance = government revenue - government expenditure.
  • A budget (fiscal) deficit means spending is greater than revenue: the answer is negative.
  • A budget (fiscal) surplus means revenue is greater than spending: the answer is positive.
  • Keep the units and currency of the data (€m, CAD$bn, Rs crore).

Before you begin

  • Government revenue and spending
  • Negative numbers
1

The method

  1. Take total government revenue.

    Why: The balance starts from what the government receives.

  2. Subtract total government expenditure and write the subtraction.

    Why: Earns the working mark.

  3. Keep the sign: negative is a deficit, positive is a surplus.

    Why: The sign shows which it is.

  4. Give the answer with the currency and units of the table.

    Why: The answer mark needs them.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Subtracting revenue from spending, which reverses the sign.
  • ✗
    Adding revenue and spending.
  • ✗
    Leaving out the currency or units.

Common mistakes: Inferred, not measured. Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

How each command word is answered

Command wordTimes askedWhat to write
Calculate3Write revenue - expenditure, then the signed balance with units, saying deficit or surplus.
multiple-choice1Subtract spending from revenue; options that add the two figures or reverse the sign are traps.

Worked explanation

  1. Revenue = Rs 2,080,201 crore; expenditure = Rs 2,784,200 crore.
  2. Fiscal balance = 2,080,201 - 2,784,200 = -703,999.
  3. The answer is negative, so India had a fiscal deficit.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Fiscal balance = revenue - expenditure = Rs 2,080,201 crore - Rs 2,784,200 crore = -Rs 703,999 crore, a fiscal deficit.

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for revenue minus spending with the figures. One for -703,999 Rs crore with the minus sign and currency. The right answer alone also earns both.
