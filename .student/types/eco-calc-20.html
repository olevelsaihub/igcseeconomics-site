Knowledge card

Total revenue from direct or indirect taxes

Calculation
  • A direct tax is paid on income or profits, straight to the government: income tax, business (corporation) tax.
  • An indirect tax is paid on spending, collected through sellers: VAT/sales tax, excise duties, customs duties and tariffs.
  • Total from one group = add only the taxes in that group.
  • Keep the units of the table, such as £bn.

Before you begin

  • Meaning of direct and indirect taxes
  • Adding decimals
1

The method

  1. Label each tax direct (on income or profit) or indirect (on spending).

    Why: Only one group is wanted.

  2. Add only the taxes in the group asked for, writing the sum.

    Why: The sum shows your selection and earns the first mark.

  3. Give the total in the table's units.

    Why: The answer mark needs the unit.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Counting income tax as indirect, or VAT as direct.
  • ✗
    Forgetting excise or customs duties as indirect taxes.
  • ✗
    Leaving out the units.

Common mistakes: Inferred, not measured. Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

Worked explanation

  1. Indirect taxes (on spending): sales tax/VAT £14.3bn, excise duties £3.7bn, customs duties £0.4bn.
  2. Income tax and business taxes are direct, so leave them out.
  3. Total = £14.3bn + £3.7bn + £0.4bn = £18.4bn.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Indirect taxes = VAT £14.3bn + excise duties £3.7bn + customs duties £0.4bn = £18.4bn.

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for adding the three indirect taxes. One for £18.4bn with its units. The correct total alone also earns both.

Calculation check

  • round(14.3+3.7+0.4, 1) = 18.4
