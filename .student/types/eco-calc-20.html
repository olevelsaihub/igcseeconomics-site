Calculation

Direct taxes are paid on income or profit. Indirect taxes are paid on spending: VAT, excise and customs duties. Add only the group asked for, in the units of the table.

The calculation

Group total = sum of the taxes in that group only

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

  • VAT is indirect; income tax is direct.
  • Excise and customs duties are indirect.
  • Keep the units, such as $bn.

Try itIncome tax $9.4bn, VAT $6.8bn, excise $2.1bn, tariffs $0.6bn. Find the indirect total.

  1. Indirect: VAT, excise, tariffs.
  2. 6.8 + 2.1 + 0.6 = 9.5
  3. $9.5bn

The method

  1. Label each tax

    Direct (income or profit) or indirect (spending).

  2. Add one group

    Write the sum of only that group.

  3. Give units

    Use the units of the table.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Swapped groups

    Income tax is direct; VAT is indirect.

  • ✗
    Missed duties

    Excise and customs duties are indirect.

  • ✗
    No units

    Write $bn or the table unit.

Worked explanation

  1. Indirect taxes (on spending): sales tax/VAT £14.3bn, excise duties £3.7bn, customs duties £0.4bn.
  2. Income tax and business taxes are direct, so leave them out.
  3. Total = £14.3bn + £3.7bn + £0.4bn = £18.4bn.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Indirect taxes = VAT £14.3bn + excise duties £3.7bn + customs duties £0.4bn = £18.4bn.

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for adding the three indirect taxes. One for £18.4bn with its units. The correct total alone also earns both.
