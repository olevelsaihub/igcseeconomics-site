Knowledge card

Converting currencies with an exchange rate

Calculation
  • An exchange rate is the price of one currency in terms of another, for example £1 = €1.18.
  • To change from the first currency to the second, multiply by the rate (pounds to euros: x 1.18).
  • To change from the second currency back to the first, divide by the rate (euros to pounds: / 1.18).
  • Check that the answer makes sense: if one pound buys more than one euro, the euro amount is the bigger number.

Before you begin

  • Meaning of an exchange rate
  • Multiplying and dividing decimals
1

The method

  1. Write the rate as 1 unit of A = x units of B.

    Why: This tells you which way to work.

  2. If you are going from A to B, multiply by x; from B to A, divide by x. Write the calculation.

    Why: Getting the direction right is the whole question.

  3. Do a size check: the answer should have more units of the 'cheaper' currency.

    Why: Catches a multiply or divide slip.

  4. Round as asked and attach the new currency symbol.

    Why: The answer mark needs the right currency.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Dividing when the rate should be multiplied, or the reverse.
  • ✗
    Giving the answer in the original currency's symbol.
  • ✗
    Rounding errors when two decimal places are asked.
  • ✗
    Adding the rate to the amount.

Common mistakes: Inferred, not measured. Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. The rate is £1 = €1.18, and the price is in pounds, so we go from pounds to euros.
  2. Pounds to euros means multiply: £750 x 1.18 = €885.
  3. Check: each pound buys more than one euro, so the euro price should be bigger. €885 is bigger than 750.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Price in euros = £750 x 1.18 = €885.

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for £750 x €1.18. One for €885 with the euro sign. The right answer alone earns both.

Calculation check

  • round(750 × 1.18, 2) = 885
