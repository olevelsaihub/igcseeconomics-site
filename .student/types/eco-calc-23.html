Converting currencies with an exchange rate

Converting a price or value between currencies using a quoted exchange rate, multiplying or dividing according to the direction of the quote.

Knowledge card

Converting currencies with an exchange rate

Calculation

An exchange rate is the price of one currency in another, such as £1 = $1.30. Going from the first currency to the second, multiply; going back, divide.

1

Multiply or divide?

Decide first, then tap the ? to check.

3 steps to find

A bike costs £400. Find its price in dollars.

  1. 1 unit of A = x of B
  2. Pounds to dollars
  3. $520

1 unit of A = x of B

Read it
One pound buys 1.30 dollars.

Pounds to dollars

Direction
First currency to second, so multiply.

$520

Working
£400 × 1.30 = $520
Check
Each pound buys more than $1, so the dollar number is bigger.
0 of 3 found

2 steps to find

A hotel stay costs $650 in total. Find it in pounds.

  1. Dollars to pounds
  2. £500

Dollars to pounds

Direction
Second currency back to first, so divide.

£500

Working
$650 ÷ 1.30 = £500
Check
Fewer pounds than dollars: right way round.

Exam tipWrite the new currency's symbol on the answer.

0 of 2 found
2

The calculation

Amount in B = amount in A × rate (when 1 A = rate × B)

Going back from B to A, divide by the rate.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find B:B = A × rate

To find A:A = B ÷ rate

  • Never add the rate to the amount.
  • Use the new currency's symbol.

Try itThe rate is €1 = $1.10. Convert €250 to dollars.

  1. Euros to dollars: multiply.
  2. €250 × 1.10 = $275

5

The method

  1. Write the rate

    1 unit of A = x units of B.

  2. Choose the direction

    A to B: multiply by x. B to A: divide by x.

  3. Size check

    The cheaper currency should have the bigger number.

  4. Finish

    Round as asked and add the new currency's symbol.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong direction

    Check which currency you start in.

  • ✗
    Old symbol

    The answer is in the new currency.

  • ✗
    Adding the rate

    Only multiply or divide.

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. The rate is £1 = €1.18, and the price is in pounds, so we go from pounds to euros.
  2. Pounds to euros means multiply: £750 x 1.18 = €885.
  3. Check: each pound buys more than one euro, so the euro price should be bigger. €885 is bigger than 750.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Price in euros = £750 x 1.18 = €885.

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for £750 x €1.18. One for €885 with the euro sign. The right answer alone earns both.

Calculation check

  • round(750 × 1.18, 2) = 885
