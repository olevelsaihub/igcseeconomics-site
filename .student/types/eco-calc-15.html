Percentage share of a total, including unemployment rate

One figure as a percentage of a larger total at one time: the unemployment rate (unemployed over labour force), spending share, people in poverty, workforce share, deficit as a share of output.

Knowledge card

Percentage share of a total, including unemployment rate

Calculation

A percentage share is part ÷ whole × 100. For the unemployment rate, the part is the unemployed and the whole is the labour force, not the population.

Percentage share = part ÷ whole × 100

Unemployment rate = unemployed ÷ labour force × 100

5

The method

  1. Name the part and whole

    For unemployment, the whole is the labour force.

  2. Find the part

    If only employed is given, subtract it from the labour force.

  3. Divide, × 100

    Write the expression with the numbers.

  4. Round and add %

    Two decimal places, then the % sign.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Using the population

    Divide by the labour force.

  • ✗
    Employed as unemployed

    Subtract first.

  • ✗
    Upside-down fraction

    Part on top, whole below.

Worked explanation

  1. Unemployed = 251,000; labour force = 9,210,000.
  2. Unemployment rate = 251,000 / 9,210,000 x 100 = 2.7252...%.
  3. Round to two decimal places: 2.73%.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Unemployment rate = unemployed / labour force x 100 = 251,000 / 9,210,000 x 100 = 2.73% (to 2 d.p.).

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for 251,000 divided by 9,210,000, times 100. One for 2.73% with the % sign. A correct answer alone earns both.
