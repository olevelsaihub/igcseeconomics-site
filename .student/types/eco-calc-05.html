Knowledge card

Calculating income elasticity of demand (YED)

Calculation

Income elasticity of demand (YED) shows how demand responds when income changes. Divide the % change in quantity demanded by the % change in income; the sign and size tell you the type of good.

1

Which kind of good is it?

Decide first, then tap the ? to check.

4 readings to find

Incomes rise by 8%. Work out the YED of each good and name its type.

  1. Income goes on the bottom
  2. Luxury: YED 2.5
  3. Necessity: YED 0.25
  4. Inferior: YED −0.5

Income goes on the bottom

Why
Income is the cause, so divide by 8.

Luxury: YED 2.5

Working
20 ÷ 8 = 2.5
Type
Above 1: demand grows faster than income.

Necessity: YED 0.25

Working
2 ÷ 8 = 0.25
Type
Between 0 and 1: a normal necessity.

Inferior: YED −0.5

Working
−4 ÷ 8 = −0.5
Type
Negative: people buy less as income rises.

Exam tipKeep the minus sign. It is what shows the good is inferior.

0 of 4 found

3 readings to find

Incomes fall by 5%. Find the YED of each good.

  1. Negative income change
  2. Luxury: YED 1.8
  3. Inferior: YED −0.6

Negative income change

Watch out
Divide by −5, not 5.

Luxury: YED 1.8

Working
−9 ÷ −5 = 1.8
Type
Two minus signs give a positive YED.

Inferior: YED −0.6

Working
3 ÷ −5 = −0.6
Type
Demand rose as income fell.
0 of 3 found
2

The calculation

YED = % change in quantity demanded ÷ % change in income

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find YED:YED = %ΔQd ÷ %ΔY

To find %ΔQd:%ΔQd = YED × %ΔY

  • Income always goes on the bottom.
  • YED has no unit and no % sign.

Try itIncomes rise by 6% and demand for smartwatches rises by 11%. Find YED to two decimal places.

  1. YED = 11 ÷ 6 = 1.8333…
  2. Answer: 1.83, a luxury good.

5

The method

  1. Write both changes

    Note the % change in quantity demanded and in income, with signs.

  2. Divide

    Quantity change ÷ income change.

  3. Round

    Round as asked. No % sign.

  4. Classify

    Above 1 luxury, 0 to 1 necessity, below 0 inferior.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Income on top

    The cause, income, always goes on the bottom.

  • ✗
    0.6 called a luxury

    Between 0 and 1 is a necessity.

  • ✗
    Unitary is not luxury

    Exactly 1 is unitary; a luxury is above 1.

Worked explanation

  1. Income rose by 7% and quantity demanded rose by 11%.
  2. YED = 11 / 7 = 1.5714...
  3. Round to two decimal places: 1.57.
  4. As 1.57 is above 1, demand rises faster than income, so this is a luxury good.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

YED = % change in quantity demanded / % change in income = 11% / 7% = 1.5714 = 1.57 (to 2 d.p.).

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for showing 11 divided by 7, and one for the result 1.57 to two decimal places. A correct answer with no working still gets both.

Calculation check

  • 11 ÷ 7 = 1.5714285714285714
  • round(11 ÷ 7, 2) = 1.57
