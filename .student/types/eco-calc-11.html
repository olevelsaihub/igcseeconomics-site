Knowledge card

Labour productivity: output per worker

Calculation
  • Labour productivity is the output produced per worker in a period of time.
  • Formula: labour productivity = total output / number of workers.
  • It is measured in units of output per worker, not in money.
  • Higher productivity means each worker makes more, which can lower the cost of each unit.

Before you begin

  • Meaning of productivity
  • Simple division
1

The method

  1. Take the total output for the period.

    Why: Productivity starts from what is produced.

  2. Divide it by the number of workers.

    Why: This gives output for each worker.

  3. Pick the option in units, not dollars.

    Why: Productivity is a quantity, so money options are wrong.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Dividing workers by output, giving a tiny number.
  • ✗
    Multiplying output by workers.
  • ✗
    Choosing an option in dollars.

Common mistakes: Inferred, not measured. Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.
