Knowledge card

Revenue, costs and profit: meanings and formulas

Written

Revenue is money in from sales: TR = P × Q. Costs are fixed or variable: TC = TFC + TVC. Profit = TR − TC; AC = TC ÷ Q.

The method

  1. Formula first

    TR = P × Q; TC = TFC + TVC; AC = TC ÷ Q; profit = TR − TC.

  2. Both parts

    Say what is measured and where it comes from.

  3. Trace profit

    Cost change, revenue change, so TR − TC.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Revenue as profit

    Revenue is before costs.

  • ✗
    Wrong AC

    Use total cost, not fixed cost.

  • ✗
    Stopping short

    Link a cost rise to lower profit.

Worked explanation

  1. Recall the formula: total revenue = price x quantity sold.
  2. Part 1: say what it is (the money received by a firm).
  3. Part 2: say where it comes from (selling all of its output).
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Total revenue is the total money a firm takes in from selling all of its goods or services in a period; it is worked out as price multiplied by the quantity sold.

How the marks are earned

1 mark for saying it is money received by the firm, and 1 mark for linking it to the sales of all its products (or to price x quantity).
