Knowledge card

Wage income from an hourly rate

Calculation
  • A wage is the payment to labour for work, often set per hour.
  • A minimum wage is the lowest hourly pay an employer may legally pay.
  • Gross income (before tax) = hourly wage x hours worked.
  • Give the answer in the currency of the wage, to two decimal places when cents are involved.

Before you begin

  • Meaning of wage and minimum wage
  • Multiplying decimals
1

The method

  1. Find the hourly wage.

    Why: This is the rate paid for each hour.

  2. Multiply by the hours worked in the period and write the calculation.

    Why: Each hour earns the rate, and showing it earns the working mark.

  3. Give the answer with currency, to two decimal places.

    Why: The answer mark needs the exact amount with its currency.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Giving just the hourly rate.
  • ✗
    Decimal slips when multiplying money.
  • ✗
    Leaving out the currency sign.

Common mistakes: Inferred, not measured. Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

Worked explanation

  1. The minimum wage is €5.87 per hour and the worker works 175 hours a month.
  2. Gross income = €5.87 x 175 = €1,027.25.

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for the line €5.87 x 175. One for €1,027.25 with the euro sign. A correct answer alone also earns both marks.
