Picking out from a cost table which items vary with output and which do not, then adding the right ones.

Knowledge card

Sorting costs into fixed and variable

Calculation

Fixed costs stay the same whatever the output. Variable costs rise and fall with output. Add only the group the question asks for, and give the total in $.

Find the variable costs, then their total.

  1. Variable: flour
  2. Variable: wages per loaf
  3. Fixed: advertising
  4. Total: 4 200 + 5 400

Variable: flour

Test
More loaves need more flour.

Variable: wages per loaf

Test
Pay depends on loaves made.

Exam tipLabour is variable when it is paid by output.

Fixed: advertising

Test
It does not change with output.

Total: 4 200 + 5 400

Answer
$4 200 + $5 400 = $9 600

Find total variable cost for 200 bikes.

  1. Parts: 45 × 200
  2. Fixed: one-off machine
  3. Electricity: 4 × 200
  4. Total: 9 000 + 800

Parts: 45 × 200

Step
A rate is not a total: 45 × 200 = $9 000.

Fixed: one-off machine

Test
Bought once, whatever the output.

Electricity: 4 × 200

Step
4 × 200 = $800.

Total: 9 000 + 800

Answer

2

The calculation

Total variable cost = sum of the variable items only

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

To find TC:TC = TFC + TVC

To find TFC:TFC = TC − TVC

To find TVC:TVC = TC − TFC

  • Multiply a per-unit rate by the quantity first.
  • Adding every item gives total cost, not TVC.

Try itInvented costs: rent $2 000, materials $1 500, insurance $300, packaging $700. Find total variable cost.

  1. Variable: materials and packaging.
  2. $1 500 + $700 = $2 200
  3. Answer: $2 200

Does it score?

Exam question

Monthly costs: rent $4 000, materials $2 500, insurance $800, wages paid per item $3 100. Calculate total variable costs. (2 marks)

Decide first: would this answer earn the mark? Then tap it to check.

Tap an answer to see if it earns the mark.

5

The method

  1. Test each item

    Would it change if the firm made more?

  2. Keep one group

    Only the group the question asks for.

  3. Rates first

    Multiply per-unit costs by the quantity.

  4. Add and finish

    Write the sum, then the total with $.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Labour as fixed

    Pay per item made is variable.

  • ✗
    Advertising as variable

    Advertising and insurance are fixed.

  • ✗
    Adding everything

    That gives total cost.

Worked explanation

  1. Rent ($25,000) and insurance ($5,500) stay the same whatever is made, so they are fixed.
  2. Raw materials ($12,000) and labour paid by output ($115,000) change with output, so they are variable.
  3. Total variable costs = $12,000 + $115,000 = $127,000.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Variable costs are raw materials and labour (because labour pay depends on output). Total variable costs = $12,000 + $115,000 = $127,000 per month.

How the marks are earned

Two marks. One for adding the right two items, $12,000 + $115,000. One for the total, $127,000, with the dollar sign. The right total alone scores both.

Calculation check

  • 12000+115000 = 127000
