Idea. When an adult is paid, income tax comes straight out of their pay. When you buy a snack, V A T is already inside the price. Both are taxes, but they work differently. A direct tax is paid on income or profits: income tax and business tax. An indirect tax is paid on spending: V A T, excise duties, customs duties and tariffs. Taxes are the government's main source of revenue. A question may ask you to add up just one group.

Method. Step one, label each tax direct or indirect. Here income tax and corporation tax are direct. V A T and excise duties are indirect. Step two, add only the group asked for, and write the sum. For indirect taxes: eighteen point five plus four point five is twenty-three. Step three, give the units: twenty-three billion pounds.

Worked example. Here is a real question from June 2024. A table lists five taxes in billions of pounds. Calculate the total revenue raised by indirect taxes. Two marks. Label each one. Income tax and business taxes are direct, so leave them out. Sales tax, excise duties and customs duties are indirect. Add them. Common mistake. A common mistake is mixing the groups, such as adding income tax to V A T. Another is leaving out the units. Write billions of pounds.
