When your pocket money goes up, you might go to the cinema more and buy fewer cheap instant noodles. Your income changes what you buy. Income elasticity of demand, or Y E D, measures how much quantity demanded responds to a change in income. Divide the percentage change in quantity demanded by the percentage change in income. The sign and size tell you the type of good. Negative means inferior. Between nought and one means a normal good that is a necessity. Above one means a luxury. For a normal good, higher incomes shift demand to the right. For an inferior good, higher incomes shift demand to the left, because people switch to something they prefer.

Here is a real question from January 2023. Megabus runs cheap buses in Canada, the U S and the U K. If incomes rise by ten per cent, demand for bus travel falls by fifteen per cent. Analyse why this is useful to Megabus. Six marks. Define Y E D, then calculate it. Minus fifteen divided by ten is minus one point five. It is negative, so bus travel is an inferior good: richer people switch to cars, trains or flights. The size, one point five, is above one, so demand is very responsive. So when incomes rise, demand for Megabus journeys shifts to the left. Use one. If incomes are forecast to rise, Megabus can cut routes and buses, saving fuel and drivers' wages on empty seats. Use two. In a recession incomes fall, so more people take the bus. Megabus can add services and hire drivers in advance, and move buses between its three countries. A common mistake is calling a good with negative Y E D a normal good, just because its demand changes. Negative always means inferior.

Now you try. Incomes in a city rise by eight per cent, and demand for restaurant meals rises by twelve per cent. Calculate Y E D, name the type of good, and give one way a restaurant chain could use this. Pause here and work it out.

Y E D is twelve divided by eight, which is one point five. It is positive and above one, so restaurant meals are a luxury. As incomes rise, demand for meals rises by an even bigger percentage, so demand shifts right. The chain could open more tables and hire staff ahead of time.
