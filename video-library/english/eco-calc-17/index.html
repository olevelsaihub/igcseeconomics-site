Finding the average (mean) of data

Say your last three test scores were six, eight and ten out of ten. Your average is the score you would get if all three were equal. The mean, or average, is the sum of all the values divided by the number of values. Six plus eight plus ten is twenty-four. Three scores, so twenty-four divided by three is eight. Exam data often shows foreign direct investment, or F D I. That is money a firm from one country invests in businesses or assets in another country.

Step one, add all the values. Step two, count them, and divide the sum by the count. Write the expression. Step three, round to two decimal places and keep the unit. Here is one that needs rounding. Unemployment rates of four point two, five point one and three point eight per cent add to thirteen point one. Divide by three: four point three six six and so on. Rounded, four point three seven per cent.

Here is a real question from November 2021. Figure four shows the F D I India received from 2016 to 2019. Calculate, to two decimal places, the annual average value of F D I in billions of dollars. Two marks. Here is the data from Figure four: forty-five point one four, fifty-five point five five, sixty point nine eight and sixty-four point three seven billion dollars. Add carefully, two at a time. The sum is two hundred and twenty-six point nought four. There are four years, so divide by four. Two hundred and twenty-six point nought four divided by four is fifty-six point five one. The answer is fifty-six point five one billion dollars. A common mistake is dividing by the wrong count. Twenty sixteen to twenty nineteen is four years, not three. Another is leaving off the unit. The answer is in billions of dollars.

Now you try. The unemployment rates in three regions of a country are four point six, seven point three and five point nine per cent. Calculate, to two decimal places, the average unemployment rate. Pause here and work it out.

Here are the three rates side by side. The average will sit between the lowest and the highest. Four point six plus seven point three plus five point nine is seventeen point eight. Divide by three: five point nine three three and so on. Rounded, five point nine three per cent. Remember: add all the values, divide by how many there are, round to two decimal places, and keep the unit. Now try the practice questions.
