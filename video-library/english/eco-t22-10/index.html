Read the narration

In a football match, a referee makes sure both teams follow the same rules. Trade between countries has a referee too. The World Trade Organization, or W T O, is an international organisation of member countries. Its purpose is to promote freer trade between them. It does this in four main ways. Know what it does not do. The W T O does not lend money, write off debts, pay subsidies, regulate multinationals or fix exchange rates. Why does this matter? A tariff is a tax on imports. It shifts the supply of imports left. When the W T O gets the tariff removed, supply shifts back right, so prices fall and more is traded.

Step one, name one W T O role. Step two, say how the W T O carries it out. Step three, show the result: barriers come down, so more trade happens. Step four, apply it to the countries in the case. For a six-mark Analyse, develop two or three roles, each linked to more trade.

Here is a real question from June 2019. In 2017, the US government wanted a three hundred per cent tariff on aircraft made by Bombardier, a Canadian firm. Explain one way the W T O could promote free trade between Canada and the US. Three marks. How big is that tariff? Three hundred per cent of the price is added on top, so an aircraft would cost four times as much in the US. The role: the W T O sets trade rules and settles disputes. Canada can complain to the W T O, which acts as a referee and judges whether the tariff breaks the agreed rules. If it breaks the rules, the US can be required to remove or reduce the tariff. Canadian aircraft keep selling in the US, and trade stays free. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

A common mistake is confusing the W T O with bodies that lend money or support currencies. Another is naming a role without showing how it increases trade, or ignoring the countries in the case.

Now you try. Country A puts a fifty per cent tariff on steel from Country B. Country B says this breaks the trade rules both agreed. Explain one way the W T O could help trade between them. Pause here and work it out.

The W T O settles disputes. Country B complains, and the W T O judges the tariff against the rules. If it breaks them, Country A must remove or cut it, so steel exports from B to A can grow again. Remember: name a W T O role, say how it works, show that trade increases, and use the countries in the case. Now try the practice questions.
