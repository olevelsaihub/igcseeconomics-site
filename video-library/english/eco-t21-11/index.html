Ali has left school and applies for jobs every week, but has no offer yet. His grandmother has retired and is not looking for work. Only Ali is unemployed. Unemployment means people who are willing and able to work, and looking for a job, but cannot find one. It is measured with the I L O method. The rate is the number unemployed divided by the labour force, times one hundred. Unemployment wastes resources. On a production possibility curve, the economy produces at a point inside the curve, like U. There are five types, each with its own cause. Falling demand: cyclical. A declining industry or new technology: structural. Time of year: seasonal. Between jobs: frictional. Choosing not to work: voluntary. Cyclical unemployment comes with the economic cycle. In a recession, spending falls, so firms need fewer workers.

Step one, define: willing and able to work, but without a job. Step two, find the cause in the question. Step three, match it to the type. Step four, for Explain: give the reason, say why it leaves people without work, and apply it to the data.

Here is a real question from June 2025. In China, unemployment for sixteen to twenty-four year olds was fourteen point nine per cent. New graduates far outnumbered jobs, and most jobs were lower-skilled. Explain one reason for structural unemployment. Three marks. The reason is a skills mismatch. Graduates are trained for higher-skilled work, but most vacancies are lower-skilled. The skills do not fit the jobs. So many young graduates cannot find suitable work, which helps explain the fourteen point nine per cent rate. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

A common mistake is mixing up structural and cyclical. A permanent change, like machines replacing workers, is structural. A temporary fall in demand is cyclical. Another is dividing by the whole population. The rate uses the labour force.

Now you try. A seaside town's hotels close every winter, and staff lose their jobs until spring. Separately, a car factory replaces two hundred workers with robots. Name the type in each case, and explain one. Pause and decide.

The hotels only need staff at certain times of the year, so that is seasonal. The robots are new technology, so that is structural. Explain the robots. Machines now do the work, so those workers' skills are no longer needed. Without retraining, the two hundred may stay unemployed for a long time.
