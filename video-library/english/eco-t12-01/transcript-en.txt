Think of a café making a cup of coffee. It needs coffee beans and water, a barista to make it, a coffee machine, and an owner who set the café up. Production means turning resources into goods and services. These resources are the factors of production. Land is gifts of nature. Labour is human effort. Capital is man-made tools and machines. Enterprise is the person who organises it all and takes the risk. Wages, rent and profit are not factors. They are rewards paid to the factors. Taxes are not factors either.

Step one, ask what the item is. Something from nature is land. A person working is labour. A man-made tool is capital. The risk-taking organiser is enterprise. Step two, say what the item does, then name it. Oil is pumped from the ground, a natural resource, therefore it is land. Step three, for an Analyse, tie every factor to a real step in the case. Finish by saying why all four are needed together.

Here is a real question from June 2023. In the Canary Islands, machines filter salt out of sea water, and workers take samples to check it. Analyse how all four factors might be used to produce drinking water. Six marks. Match each detail to a factor. Sea water is land. The filter machines are capital. The workers taking samples are labour. Enterprise is not named, so explain it. Now link each one. Sea water is the raw material. The machines remove salt far faster than by hand. The workers check samples, so the water is safe to drink. Someone must buy the machines, hire the workers and risk losing money. That is enterprise. Each factor depends on the others, so all four are needed. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

A common mistake is calling profit a factor of production. Profit is the reward for enterprise. Another is calling a mechanic capital because he works with machines. He is a person, so he is labour.

Now you try. Kofi runs a fishing business. He catches fish from the sea using a boat, with two crew members. He used his own savings to start it. Name the factor for each. Pause here and work it out.

The fish come from nature, so they are land. The boat is man-made, so it is capital. The crew give human effort, so they are labour. Kofi organises and risks his savings, so he is enterprise. Remember: ask what the item is, name the factor, and link it to the case. Now try the practice questions.
