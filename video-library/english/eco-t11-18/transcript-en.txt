A firm plans a fireworks show over a town. Everyone nearby can watch, whether they paid or not. So very few people pay, and the firm makes a loss. Market failure is when a free market does not allocate resources efficiently, so society does not get the best use of its scarce resources. Its causes include public goods, externalities, lack of information, lack of competition and factor immobility. A public good has two features. It is non-excludable: you cannot stop people who do not pay from benefiting. And it is non-rival: one person using it leaves no less for others. A free rider is someone who gets the benefit of a good but pays nothing towards it.

Step one, define the term. Step two, show the good is non-excludable and non-rival. Step three, explain that people benefit without paying, so a firm earns too little. Step four, link to government provision. Other causes give other results. Lack of competition can mean higher prices. Lack of information can mean consumers pay more than they need to.

Here is a real question from June 2022. Street lighting improves safety and visibility. Analyse why the free rider problem means street lighting is provided by the government. Six marks. A free rider benefits without paying. Street lighting is non-excludable. Once a light is on, nobody can stop a pedestrian or driver from seeing by it, whether they paid or not. It is also non-rival. One driver using the light leaves just as much light for the next pedestrian. So people know they will get the safety anyway, and few will pay. A private firm earns little revenue, cannot make a profit, and will not supply it. That is a missing market. So the government provides street lighting and pays for it with taxes, which everyone must pay. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

A common mistake is saying a public good is anything the government provides. It must have the two features. Another is mixing up the two features. Non-excludable is about stopping non-payers. Non-rival is about how much is left for others.

Now you try. A coastal town needs a sea wall to protect homes from flooding. Explain why a private firm is unlikely to build it. Pause here and work it out.

The sea wall is non-excludable. Every home behind it is protected, whether its owner pays or not. So many people free ride, the firm cannot earn enough revenue, and it will not build it. The government provides it through taxes. Remember: define, show both features, explain the lack of profit, and link to the government. Now try the practice questions.
