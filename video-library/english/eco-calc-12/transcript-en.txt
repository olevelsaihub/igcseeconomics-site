Imagine a Saturday job in a café. You are paid six pounds for every hour you work. Work four hours, and you earn twenty-four pounds. These are example figures. A wage is the payment to labour for work, often set per hour. A minimum wage is the lowest hourly pay an employer may legally pay. Gross income is pay before any tax is taken off. So gross income equals the hourly wage times the hours worked. Each hour adds another six pounds: six, twelve, eighteen, twenty-four.

Step one. Find the hourly wage. Then find the hours worked in the period the question asks about, a week or a month. Step two. Multiply, and write the calculation down. For example, six pounds forty an hour for twenty hours. Six pounds forty times twenty is one hundred and twenty-eight pounds. Step three. Give the answer with the currency sign and two decimal places. Twenty hours earn far more than one hour, so a sensible answer is much bigger than the rate.

Here is a real question from January 2020. Andorra's minimum wage rose to five euros eighty-seven per hour. Calculate the monthly gross income of a worker paid the minimum wage for one hundred and seventy-five hours a month. Two marks. Pick out the two figures. The hourly wage is five euros eighty-seven. The hours are one hundred and seventy-five a month. Five euros eighty-seven times one hundred and seventy-five is one thousand and twenty-seven euros twenty-five. That is the monthly gross income. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme. Check it quickly. About six euros times one hundred and seventy-five is one thousand and fifty euros. Our answer is close, so there is no decimal slip.

A common mistake is giving just the hourly rate. The question asks for a month's pay, so multiply by the hours. Another is a decimal slip, or leaving out the currency sign. Estimate first, and write the euro sign.

Now you try. In an example country, a café worker is paid the minimum wage of seven pounds forty-five an hour, and works thirty-two hours a week. Calculate the weekly gross income. Pause here and work it out.

Seven pounds forty-five times thirty-two is two hundred and thirty-eight pounds forty. See how it builds. Ten hours earn seventy-four pounds fifty. Thirty hours earn two hundred and twenty-three pounds fifty. Thirty-two hours earn two hundred and thirty-eight pounds forty. Remember: find the rate and the hours, multiply and show it, then give the currency and two decimal places. Now try the practice questions.
