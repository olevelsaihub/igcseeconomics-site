Imagine one big pizza. Shared by two friends, each gets a lot. Shared by ten, each gets a thin slice. A country's output works the same way. Two countries each produce one hundred billion dollars. Country A has ten million people, so ten thousand dollars each. Country B has one hundred million people, so only one thousand dollars each. So G D P per capita is G D P divided by the population: the average output per person. First, put both numbers in the same units. One billion is one thousand million.

Step one, check the units. Here G D P is four point five billion dollars and the population is three million. Write G D P as four thousand five hundred million. Step two, divide G D P by population and write it down. Four thousand five hundred divided by three is one thousand five hundred. Step three, give the currency: one thousand five hundred dollars per person.

Here is a real question from June 2019. It gives Vietnam's population and G D P in 2016. Calculate G D P per capita to two decimal places. Two marks. Population is ninety-two point seven million. G D P is two hundred and two thousand six hundred million dollars. Both are in millions, so the millions cancel. Two hundred and two thousand six hundred divided by ninety-two point seven is two thousand one hundred and eighty-five point five four four. To two decimal places, that is two thousand one hundred and eighty-five dollars fifty-four. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme. So on average, each person in Vietnam produced about two thousand one hundred and eighty-five dollars of output in 2016.

A common mistake is dividing the wrong way round. Population divided by G D P gives a tiny, meaningless number. Another is mixing billions and millions. Five billion divided by two million is two thousand five hundred dollars, not two dollars fifty.
