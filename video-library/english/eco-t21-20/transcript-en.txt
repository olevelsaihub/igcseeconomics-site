Idea. One family cannot afford enough food or a safe home. Another family has food and a home, but far less than most families around them. Both are poor, in different ways. Absolute poverty means income is too low to pay for basic needs for survival: food, clean water and shelter. Relative poverty means a living standard below the typical standard in that society. The E U often uses income below sixty per cent of the median. Income inequality means income is spread unevenly, so there is a gap between groups. Here one household earns nine times as much as another. Why do governments act? To make sure basic needs are met, to raise living standards, and for fairness.

Method. Step one, decide which idea is asked. Step two, give both parts of the definition. For inequality, say income is unevenly shared, and between which groups. Step three, for a reason to act: name it, develop it, and use the case.

Worked example. Here is a real question from January 2023. The Scottish Government is giving thirty-seven point two million pounds to low-income households to tackle poverty and inequality. Explain one reason why it wants to reduce them. Three marks. Pick one reason: to make sure basic needs are met. Low-income households may not afford food, heating or a safe home. Apply it. The thirty-seven point two million pounds helps these families pay for essentials, so fewer people in Scotland live in poverty. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is swapping the two types. Can't meet basic needs is absolute. Below the typical standard is relative. Another is a vague definition. Being poor earns nothing. Say what it is compared with.

Your turn. Now you try. In Country K, the typical family earns nine hundred dollars a month. The Lee family earns three hundred and fifty. They can pay for food and rent, but little else. Absolute or relative poverty? Give one reason the government may help. Pause and decide.

Answer. They meet basic needs, so it is not absolute poverty. But sixty per cent of nine hundred is five hundred and forty, and they earn far less. This is relative poverty. One reason: to raise living standards. With so little left after rent and food, the Lees cannot afford things most families have. Help would lift their living standard closer to the typical level. Remember: decide which idea, give both parts of the definition, and for reasons: name, develop, apply. Now try the practice questions.
