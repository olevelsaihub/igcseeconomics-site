For unemployment, the whole is the labour force: everyone in work, plus everyone looking for work. Retired people and young children are not in it. So the unemployment rate is the number unemployed, divided by the labour force, times one hundred.

Here is a real question from June 2023. The Netherlands had a labour force of nine million two hundred and ten thousand, and two hundred and fifty-one thousand unemployed. Calculate the rate of unemployment, to two decimal places. Two marks. The part is the unemployed: two hundred and fifty-one thousand. The whole is the labour force: nine million two hundred and ten thousand. Two hundred and fifty-one thousand divided by nine million two hundred and ten thousand, times one hundred, is two point seven two five. To two decimal places, two point seven three per cent. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme. So fewer than three in every hundred people in the labour force were unemployed.
